Atoms & Earth Quest

Cross three wild lands and face the Ember Dragon. Collect three Knowledge Scrolls in each land to open its gate. If a creature catches you, answer its question correctly and it runs away. Answer wrongly and you'll have to fight it.

Caught by a creature?Right answer: it flees. Wrong answer: it strikes first and you fight.
FightingMove in with →, strike with Space, and step back with ← when the enemy winds up to attack.
