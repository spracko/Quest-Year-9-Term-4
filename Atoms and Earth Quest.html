Atoms & Earth Quest

Cross three wild lands and face the Ember Dragon. Collect three Knowledge Scrolls in each land to open its gate. If a creature catches you, answer its question correctly and it runs away. Answer wrongly and you'll have to fight it.

    For teachers

    Question bank

    One question per line, with parts separated by |. There are three kinds:
    Multiple choice: chapter | difficulty 1–4 | question | CORRECT answer | wrong 1 | wrong 2 | wrong 3 | explanation (the correct answer goes first; the game shuffles the options)
    Vocabulary: V | chapter | difficulty | definition | answer;other accepted answer | explanation (capitals, spaces and hyphens are ignored; spelling must match)
    Numerical: N | chapter | difficulty | question | answer | allowed error (0 = exact) | unit | explanation
    Lines starting with # are ignored. Saved changes stay in this browser only. To change the bank for everyone, edit the DEFAULT_BANK section near the top of the game's script.
